Culprit watch-list & easy upgrades

Item (category) Why it's on the list Simple improvement
Organic Reduced-Sugar Strawberry Preserve (condiment) Added sugar is still #2 ingredient; 7 g per Tbsp Swap to freezer-aisle whole-fruit purée or mash fresh berries with chia seeds—same flavour, 60% less sugar

Organic Low-Fat Cottage Cheese (dairy) Carrageenan thickener + salt Choose a "no stabiliser" brand or strained Greek cottage cheese to drop gums & lower sodium
💰 Money matters
  • Protein drives the bill. Egg-white cartons ≈ $20 and turkey ≈ $6 (51% of spend). Bulk 32-oz egg-white cartons or buying turkey in a family-pack can shave ~$4 per week.
  • Condiments cost more than produce. Three condiments ($12) vs. two fruit packs ($8). Use one condiment till empty before buying another to cut waste and cash.
📈 Smart tweaks for next week
  • Add two veggie-forward items (bagged salad, baby carrots) to balance the protein load and boost potassium.
  • Finish or freeze one open condiment before cracking a new jar—reduces both calories and grocery costs.
  • Cap high-sodium items at one per main meal; pair each with ≥ 16 oz plain water.
  • Keep the protein streak but diversify—trade one egg-white carton for a 6-oz Greek-yogurt cup to add calcium and probiotics.

Swapping out the handful of "culprit" items can slash added sugar by > 40%, drop sodium by ~600 mg per day, and save roughly $7 without losing the convenience you like. 🚀
